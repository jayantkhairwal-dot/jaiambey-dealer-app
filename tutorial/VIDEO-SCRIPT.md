# Jai Ambey Dealer App – Tutorial video (Hinglish, motion graphics)

**Length:** about 2 minutes · **Format:** 1920×1080 (16:9), 60 fps · **Voice:** Hinglish
**App screens to use:** `tutorial/screens/` (real screenshots of the app, one per step; file names are in each scene)

> AI video tools often garble small UI text if they draw the app themselves.
> Always give the tool the real screenshot of the scene as the image/reference input and ask it to
> **animate around it** (phone mockup, zoom, finger tap, highlight) **without redrawing the screen**.

---

## 1. Master prompt (paste once, at the start or in "style / global settings")

```
Create a premium motion-graphics tutorial video for a mobile ordering app, 1920x1080, 16:9, 60 fps,
smooth and crisp, total length about 2 minutes.

BRAND & LOOK
- Brand: "Jai Ambey Accessories" – car accessories wholesaler in Karol Bagh, New Delhi, 20+ years.
- Background: warm near-black #08070A with a slow, glowing red light behind the phone – like a curved
  planet horizon / soft aurora, deep red #D73630 / #E5352B fading to warm orange-red #FF6A4D with a soft
  white core. Subtle film grain, no clutter.
- A modern black smartphone (thin bezels, rounded corners) floats centre or slightly left, with a soft
  red rim light and a gentle reflection under it.
- The phone screen shows ONLY the screenshot I provide for each scene. Never redraw, translate, blur or
  change any text, price, button or photo on the screen. Keep it sharp and readable.
- Typography for on-screen captions: bold geometric sans (like SF Pro Display / Inter / Poppins Bold),
  white, tight letter spacing; small grey (#B8B0B4) sub-captions. Captions sit on the right third.
- Highlight colour: brand red #E5352B; success colour: green #25D366 (only for "order placed").

MOTION
- Apple-keynote style: slow push-ins, smooth 3D phone tilts (max 12°), parallax background glow.
- Easing: ease-in-out (cubic), no jerky moves, no fast spins. Transitions 0.4–0.6 s: soft zoom-through
  or slide with motion blur.
- Show taps with a soft white finger-tap ripple (circle that expands and fades) exactly on the button
  being explained, then a red rounded-rectangle highlight around that button.
- Zoom into the important area (button, price, search bar) up to 1.6x, hold 1.5 s, zoom back.
- Captions animate in word-by-word (slide up 12 px + fade), out with a fade.

AUDIO
- Hinglish voiceover, warm and friendly male or female Indian voice, clear and slow (about 130 words
  per minute), like a shop owner explaining to another shop owner.
- Light, modern, positive background music (lo-fi / corporate tech), ducked under the voice.
- Soft UI sounds: tap "click" on every tap, a light "whoosh" on transitions, a pleasant chime on
  "Order placed".

AVOID
- No fake app screens, no invented buttons, prices, phone numbers or UPI IDs.
- No people's faces, no stock-footage people, no 3D cartoon characters.
- No text on screen other than the captions given per scene.
- No flicker, no warped phone, no melting text, no watermark.
```

## 2. Scene-by-scene script

Each scene: **Screen** = screenshot to upload · **Motion** = what to animate · **Caption** = on-screen text ·
**Voiceover** = Hinglish line to speak.

### Scene 1 – Intro (0:00–0:08)
- **Screen:** `01-home.jpg`
- **Motion:** Black screen → red glow rises like a sunrise → phone fades in from below and tilts to face the
  viewer. Jai Ambey logo appears big, then shrinks to the top-left corner for the rest of the video.
- **Caption:** **Jai Ambey Accessories – Dealer App** / *Order karna ab aur aasaan*
- **Voiceover:** "Namaste! Ab Jai Ambey Accessories se order karna hai bilkul aasaan. Dekhiye, sirf do minute mein, app kaise use karna hai."

### Scene 2 – Open the app (0:08–0:18)
- **Screen:** `01-home.jpg`
- **Motion:** Slow push-in on the phone. Small animated icon: a link → Chrome → "Add to Home screen" → app icon
  lands on a phone home screen (simple flat icons, drawn by the tool).
- **Caption:** **Ek link. Koi download nahi.** / *Chrome → ⋮ → Add to Home screen*
- **Voiceover:** "App ek link se khulta hai, koi download nahi. Link Chrome mein kholiye, upar teen dots dabaiye, aur Add to Home screen kar lijiye. Ab app ek tap mein khulega."

### Scene 3 – Categories & favourites (0:18–0:30)
- **Screen:** `02-categories.jpg`, then `03-favourites.jpg`
- **Motion:** Phone screen scrolls (cross-dissolve between the two screenshots, vertical slide). Red highlight boxes
  pop on each category tile one after another (Dashboard, Hanging, Spray, Solar). Then a horizontal swipe gesture on
  Dealer favourites.
- **Caption:** **4 categories** – Dashboard · Hanging · Spray · Solar / **Dealer favourites** – sabse zyada bikne wale
- **Voiceover:** "Home par chaar categories hain: Dashboard, Hanging, Spray aur Solar. Aur Dealer favourites mein woh products hain jo dealers sabse zyada order karte hain."

### Scene 4 – Search by name or code (0:30–0:40)
- **Screen:** `04-catalog.jpg`, then `05-search.jpg`
- **Motion:** Zoom into the search bar (1.6x). Typing animation of "BO-15" appears as a caption bubble next to the
  phone (not on the screen), then switch to `05-search.jpg` showing the result. Highlight the result card.
- **Caption:** **Naam ya code se search** / *jaise BO-15*
- **Voiceover:** "Catalog mein kisi bhi perfume ko naam ya code se dhoondhiye. Jaise BO-15 likha, aur product turant saamne."

### Scene 5 – Product page & quantity (0:40–0:52)
- **Screen:** `06-product.jpg`, then `07-added.jpg`
- **Motion:** Push-in on the quantity row; tap ripple on **12**; highlight the total "₹3,000". Then tap on the white
  button → switch to `07-added.jpg`, small check-mark burst.
- **Caption:** **Quantity: 6 · 12 · 24 · 48** / *ya – + se apni quantity*
- **Voiceover:** "Product kholiye. Quantity ke liye seedha 6, 12, 24 ya 48 dabaiye, ya plus-minus se apni quantity. Total apne aap dikh jaata hai. Add to cart dabaiye. Ho gaya!"

### Scene 6 – Cart bar (0:52–0:59)
- **Screen:** `08-cartbar.jpg`
- **Motion:** Bottom white bar slides up with a bounce; highlight **View cart**.
- **Caption:** **View cart** – total hamesha neeche
- **Voiceover:** "Neeche total aur items hamesha dikhte hain. Jab ready ho, View cart dabaiye."

### Scene 7 – Sold out & On request (0:59–1:09)
- **Screen:** `09-onrequest.jpg`, then `10-soldout.jpg`
- **Motion:** Split view: phone left with the On request cards, small tag animation "ON REQUEST" pulses; then
  the Sold out page with the tag "SOLD OUT" highlighted.
- **Caption:** **SOLD OUT** – stock aate hi wapas / **ON REQUEST** – "Ask for it" dabaiye
- **Voiceover:** "Sold out matlab abhi stock nahi, stock aate hi wapas mil jaayega. On request wale products hum aapke liye arrange karte hain. Bas Ask for it dabaiye."

### Scene 8 – Cart & shop details (1:09–1:20)
- **Screen:** `11-cart.jpg`, then `12-details.jpg`
- **Motion:** Highlight the – + buttons and the total; then slide to the form, fields highlight one by one.
- **Caption:** **Shop details – sirf pehli baar**
- **Voiceover:** "Cart mein quantity badal sakte hain. Pehli baar apna naam, shop ka naam, city aur mobile number bhariye. Yeh save ho jaata hai, agli baar nahi bharna."

### Scene 9 – Pay by UPI (1:20–1:34)
- **Screen:** `13-pay-upi.jpg`, then `14-qr.jpg`
- **Motion:** Highlight **Pay by UPI app**; small animated icons of UPI apps (generic, no brand logos) pop out of
  the button. Then the QR code scales up next to the phone with a scan-line animation.
- **Caption:** **Step 1: UPI se pay** / *Pay by UPI app · UPI ID copy · QR code*
- **Voiceover:** "Ab payment. Pay by UPI app dabaiye, ya UPI ID copy kijiye, ya QR code scan kijiye. Amount daaliye aur UPI PIN. Bas."

### Scene 10 – UTR & screenshot = order placed (1:34–1:50)
- **Screen:** `15-utr.jpg`, then `16-placed.jpg`
- **Motion:** Zoom on the UTR box; a small "payment receipt" card (generic) slides in beside the phone with the
  12-digit number glowing, and the number flies into the UTR box. Then tap on "Add screenshot & place order" →
  hard cut to `16-placed.jpg` with a green check-mark burst and soft confetti in brand red/white.
- **Caption:** **Step 2: UTR number** / **Step 3: Screenshot daalo → Order placed!**
- **Voiceover:** "Payment ke baad apne UPI app mein UTR number dekhiye, yeh 12 ankon ka hota hai, aur yahan likhiye. Phir payment ka screenshot daaliye. Screenshot daalte hi, order apne aap place! Hamari team bank mein payment check karke order dispatch karti hai, aur bilty aapko WhatsApp par milti hai."

### Scene 11 – My orders & Reorder (1:50–2:02)
- **Screen:** `17-orders.jpg`
- **Motion:** Highlight the order card, then a big tap ripple on **Reorder**; items fly from the order card into a
  cart icon.
- **Caption:** **Reorder – ek tap mein wahi order**
- **Voiceover:** "Agli baar? My orders mein jaiye aur Reorder dabaiye. Wahi products, wahi quantity, ek tap mein."

### Scene 12 – Outro (2:02–2:10)
- **Screen:** `18-about.jpg`
- **Motion:** Phone slowly turns away, red glow grows, logo returns to centre.
- **Caption:** **Jai Ambey Accessories** / *Karol Bagh, New Delhi · 20+ saal ka bharosa*
- **Voiceover:** "Jai Ambey Accessories. Bees saal ka bharosa, ab aapke phone par. Aaj hi order kijiye!"

## 4. Export settings
- Resolution 1920×1080, 16:9, **60 fps**, H.264 (High profile) or H.265, bitrate 16–20 Mbps (VBR 2-pass), AAC 320 kbps.
- Safe area: keep captions inside the central 90%.
- Also export a 1080×1920 (9:16) version for WhatsApp Status / Instagram Reels: phone full-height in the centre,
  captions above and below.
- Add Hinglish subtitles (burned in or .srt) – many dealers watch on mute.

## 5. Before sharing with dealers
- The UPI ID in `13-pay-upi.jpg`, `14-qr.jpg` is a placeholder (`jaiambey@xxxx`). Retake the screenshots after the real
  business UPI ID is in the app.
- Payment is not switched on for dealers yet; release the video together with the switch-on.
